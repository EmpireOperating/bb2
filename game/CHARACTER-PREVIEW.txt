Character:
  Company > choose person > Character opens the endorsed original attribute
  table and stat training. That entire table/training block is byte-identical.
  The compact summary shows service, health/injuries, next-rank progress,
  temperament, sustain and equipment. Details retains complete history, bonds,
  effective/permanent stats, wounds/scars and gear in a read-only scroll.
  Close/Escape returns to the same person and Company context.

Perks:
  Company > Perks and Character > Personal Perks open the same original tree.
  Tiers are centered. Active filter matches have gold wash/outline/diamond;
  nonmatches stay inspectable and eligibility is unchanged. +N on Perks means
  earned unspent perk points. Stat training is on Character, not this tree.

Founders and talent:
  New companies have three Companions: 2H, Shield and Ranged, with researched
  Battle Brothers baseline talents/ranges and modest game-specific gear and
  compatible perk affinities. Ordinary recruit talents select three eligible
  stats uniformly; Deserters exclude Resolve. Selected stats receive 1/2/3
  stars at 60/30/10, not invented favored-background weighting.

Shops:
  Town equipment and Company market show actual catalogue properties and
  clearly identify catalogue bases. Actual owned rolls are assigned on buying;
  inspection does not purchase, reroll or equip.
